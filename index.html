Privacy Policy — PDF Maker - Image to PDF

Last updated: 21 October 2026

PDF Maker ("the app") processes PDF documents and images on your device. This policy describes what the app does and doesn't do with your data.

1. We do not collect your documents

  • All PDF operations — image-to-PDF, merge, split, compress, PDF-to-images, organize, scan, watermark, page numbers, signature, password protect / unlock, OCR, metadata — run on your Android device.
  • The app does not upload your documents, images, or extracted text to any server.
  • The app does not store PDF passwords you type.
  • The app has no login, no account, no cloud sync.

2. What the app stores locally

  • Theme preference (light / dark / system).
  • Chosen language (English / Hindi / Marathi, or system default).
  • A list of the files you recently processed with the app — path, name, size, and timestamp. No document content.
  • Which tools you have marked as favorites.

This is kept in Android's local SharedPreferences for your account only. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Permissions and why we need them

  • CAMERA — the built-in document scanner and "take a photo". Optional.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below) — opening files you choose in the system picker.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and below only) — "Save to phone" into Downloads / Pictures. Android 10+ needs no permission for this.
  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — loading ads only. Never used for your documents.

Images and PDFs are chosen through Android's system picker, so the app has no broad access to your photos. We do not request MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE ("All files access").

4. Third-party services

The app uses the following services. Each one may collect data per its own policy; the app does not share your documents with any of them:

  • Google AdMob — serves ads inside the app. AdMob may collect limited device and ad-interaction data per its privacy policy.
  • Google ML Kit (text recognition) — runs OCR on your device. The on-device recognizer does not upload images.
  • Google ML Kit document scanner — provided by Google Play services. Scanning and cropping run on your device; Google Play services may download the scanner module once.
  • Google Play In-App Review — shows Google's rating dialog occasionally. Ratings are handled by Google Play.
  • Syncfusion Flutter PDF — a client-side PDF library that runs locally. No network traffic.

5. Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly process children's personal information.

6. Changes to this policy

If we materially change how the app handles data, this document and the in-app Privacy Policy screen will be updated.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/awares2011-ship-it/pdf-maker-privacy-policy/issues.
